Q&A Highlights
Community asks,
speakers answer
❓ Using ChatGPT but productivity isn't improving?
The problem isn't the tool, it's the problem definition. Assign bigger, multi-platform challenges — that's when AI gets fully utilized.
❓ Can a 5–10 person team benefit from AI?
Smaller teams have the advantage — faster decisions, lower cost. A 3-person team can operate 1,000+ virtual AI accounts.
❓ Can AI handle empathy-heavy industries (spa, beauty, travel)?
Yes, if you start from business scripts. Train AI like a new employee — proven in spa, healthcare, and travel verticals.
